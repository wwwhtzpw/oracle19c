Required Postinstallation Tasks

Download and apply required patches for your software release after completing your initial installation.

  • Downloading Release Update Patches
    Download and install Release Updates (RU) and Monthly Recommended Patches (MRPs) patches for your Oracle software after you complete installation.
  • Unlocking and Resetting Oracle Database User Passwords
    Passwords for all Oracle system administration accounts except SYS, SYSTEM, and DBSMP are revoked after installation. Before you use a locked account, you must unlock it and reset its password.
