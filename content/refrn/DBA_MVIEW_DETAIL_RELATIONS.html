5.190 DBA_MVIEW_DETAIL_RELATIONS

DBA_MVIEW_DETAIL_RELATIONS represents the named detail relations that are either in the FROM list of a materialized view, or that are indirectly referenced through views in the FROM list. Its columns are the same as those in ALL_MVIEW_DETAIL_RELATIONS.
